# 0.5 calibration and hardening execution plan

## Status and outcome

Status: planned after the 0.4 exit gate.

The phase outcome is a calibrated selective-risk system with multiple generation
strategies, explicit risk routing, qualified partial atomicity, and retained
evidence that additional complexity improves useful coverage or style without
weakening the fidelity bound.

This phase does not turn probabilistic assessment into a semantic proof. It decides
which outputs are accepted at a predeclared risk policy and reports how much useful
work the system declines.

## Entry criteria

- Plain text, profiles, and the supported Markdown subset pass their release gates.
- Model, evaluator, prompt, and benchmark artifacts have immutable identities.
- Evaluation governance separates development, calibration, locked, smoke, and red
  team suites.
- Human annotation and adjudication instructions are versioned and approved.
- Numeric thresholds and stop conditions can be registered before the locked run.

## Required decisions

Accept decision records for:

- Metric definitions and statistical intervals
- Semantic evaluator ensemble and calibration method
- Per-risk-category uncertainty and threshold policy
- Strategy contracts and router inputs
- Candidate count, diversity, retry, and compute budgets
- Mode-specific edit-cost and divergence bands
- Unit and region atomicity consistency policy
- Human adjudication and model-assisted triage boundaries
- Benchmark-data governance covering storage, encryption, authorized principals,
  access-log retention, backups, participant revocation, suite sealing, disclosure,
  retirement, and replacement
- Mutation testing scope and minimum score policy
- Profile storage encryption decision if it was not closed in 0.3
- Portable runtime process, artifact conversion, backend identity, device fallback,
  and no-silent-downgrade policy
- 1.0 language matrix, mixed-language scope, native review, and per-language
  statistical gates
- Editorial-lint rule schema, context exclusions, policy precedence, and separation
  from source-signal diagnostics

## Evaluation governance

Each benchmark manifest declares one suite kind:

- `smoke`: fast contract and installation checks
- `development`: visible cases used to repair behavior
- `calibration`: threshold and calibration fitting only
- `locked`: untouched release evidence
- `red_team`: adversarial discovery and attack exercises

The manifest records content digest, provenance, license, split-generation revision,
seed, annotation rubric, adjudication digest, case inclusions and exclusions,
baseline identities, exact system artifacts, hardware, operating system,
pre-registered thresholds, and statistical method.

Locked cases never become prompt examples, tuning data, or detailed regression
fixtures. A label defect creates a new suite version and invalidation record. A
confirmed behavioral defect receives a minimized development fixture without
copying the locked case into ordinary tuning data.

Locked data is access-controlled according to the approved data policy. Its manifest
digest is visible, while case access is least-privileged and logged. Public
disclosure or access broad enough to make repeated tuning likely retires the suite
and requires a newly sealed locked version.

## Metric contract

Report at least these distinct measures:

- Corruption acceptance: corrupt candidates accepted divided by corrupt candidates
  evaluated
- Accepted-set semantic error: corrupt accepted candidates divided by all accepted
  candidates
- Eligible-candidate coverage: acceptable candidates accepted divided by acceptable
  candidates
- System transformation coverage: eligible documents actually rewritten divided by
  eligible documents
- Abstention rate and stable reason distribution
- Owner style preference on the common accepted subset where both compared systems
  return an output
- One-sided and two-sided abstention outcomes
- Coverage-adjusted product utility under a pre-registered scoring rule
- Exact invariant and structural failure rates
- Latency, cancellation latency, memory, disk, and energy observations

Accepted-set error depends on the test mixture, so every report states category
prevalence. Results are stratified by risk, mode, channel, format, artifact,
quantization, runtime, language, hardware, and operating system. Every required
stratum has a predeclared minimum sample size. A smaller stratum reports
`insufficient evidence` with its interval and is never silently omitted.

Every case carries cluster IDs for shared source, template, topic, participant, and
other dependence created by suite construction. The protocol predeclares the
analysis unit. Use one independent cluster outcome for exact binomial bounds or a
qualified cluster-aware interval when dependence remains. Do not pool weak categories
into a strong aggregate or tune on the locked set.

## Ordered work packages

### 1. Expand and lock evaluation data

- Add independently reviewed acceptable paraphrases and minimal corruptions for
  every risk category.
- Add cross-sentence, cross-block, and profile-retrieval failures.
- Add prompt injection, evidence extraction, Unicode, control, context-boundary,
  repetition, malformed backend, model drift, and correlated evaluator failures.
- Balance case prevalence deliberately and document the relationship to expected
  real use.
- Run duplicate, leakage, label-consistency, and split-contamination checks.
- Freeze calibration and locked manifests before threshold work.

Human adjudicators remain the release-label authority. Model judges may triage or
surface disagreement but cannot be the sole source of fidelity or preference labels.

### 2. Define semantic signals independently

Evaluate signals for:

- Typed literals and protected values
- Novel entities and quantities
- Entity roles and relationships
- Polarity and negation
- Modality and obligation
- Conditions and exceptions
- Attribution and quotation scope
- Temporal ordering and dates
- Clause alignment
- Bidirectional entailment and contradiction
- Cross-unit and document-level consistency

Every signal has a precise raw definition, artifact identity, applicable scope,
failure behavior, and independent performance report. A textual self-reported model
confidence is not used as calibrated evidence.

### 3. Calibrate pass, fail, and uncertain

- Fit calibration only on the calibration split.
- Version calibration by evaluator artifact, runtime, prompt, parameters, language,
  mode, and risk distribution.
- Report reliability, discrimination, and risk-coverage behavior.
- Test threshold stability under category and length shift.
- Map invalid, missing, timed-out, or unsupported calibration strata to uncertain.
- Introduce an out-of-distribution detector only through a separate qualification
  that defines its signal, threshold, supported strata, false-positive and
  false-negative evaluation, and failure behavior.
- Make strict policies reject uncertain.
- Record threshold changes as new policy versions with compatibility effects.

Using the generator as evaluator remains an ablation unless it independently meets
the strict-mode evidence bar and correlated failures are bounded.

### 4. Add the literal strategy

- Apply declared deterministic edits only.
- Invoke no generative model and require no learned semantic score.
- Keep typed literal, adapter structure, safety, and declared-rule gates.
- Produce deterministic plans, traces, and results.
- Measure the strategy as a low-risk coverage floor.

Literal mode cannot silently fall through to a generative strategy.

### 5. Add the constrained strategy

- Permit bounded local wording changes and no sentence reordering.
- Use one rewrite unit plus explicitly bounded local context.
- Keep exact sentinel restoration and the complete validation cascade.
- Use smaller candidate and output budgets than grounded generation.
- Abstain when safe local context cannot resolve references or relationships.

Qualify constrained generation separately by mode, model, and risk category.

### 6. Keep render experimental

- Place whole-block reconstruction behind an explicit experimental flag.
- Restrict it to formats and blocks with unambiguous reassembly behavior.
- Apply stricter document-level and divergence checks.
- Exclude it from stable defaults and support claims until it improves an important
  outcome under the same risk bound.
- Never use it to bypass unsupported adapter syntax.

### 7. Implement a monotonic risk router

Router inputs include entity and quantity density, negation, modality, conditions,
comparatives, citations, identifiers, coreference, sentence count, tables, cross-unit
references, adapter capabilities, profile evidence, and evaluator availability.

The router:

- Uses a checked-in partial order over strategy eligibility, fidelity floors,
  required gates, context exposure, candidate budgets, and atomicity.
- Selects only strategies permitted by the requested mode.
- Can increase validation strictness but cannot lower the fidelity floor.
- Chooses the stricter path when risk features conflict or are uncertain.
- Records features, policy version, decision, and alternatives in the trace.
- Remains deterministic for identical analyzed input and policy.

Router tests cover every boundary, conflicting feature, missing signal, and strategy
availability combination. Property tests prove that increased risk or missing
evidence never moves downward in the partial order.

### 8. Bound candidate diversity and retries

- Generate diversity through explicit parameter or prompt variants, not unbounded
  repeated sampling.
- Freeze candidate count and order, context budget, seed schedule, evaluator cascade,
  stopping rules, and total compute for each qualified system policy.
- Allocate a total per-document work budget across units and candidates.
- Retry only typed transient transport failures.
- Never retry a deterministic validation failure with the same candidate.
- Prohibit validation-triggered extra generation outside the frozen schedule unless
  the complete adaptive policy is separately qualified at system level.
- Do not let retries change model, backend, privacy, network, or fidelity policy.
- Stop immediately on cancellation, artifact drift, or resource exhaustion.
- Record candidate lineage and retry reason without raw candidate text by default.

Any increase in candidate count must show measured coverage or style gain relative to
latency, memory, and error-correlation cost.

### 9. Calibrate edit cost and ranking

Hard gates and disallowed uncertainty eliminate candidates first. Among eligible
candidates, rank lexicographically by declared constraints, style, channel fit,
fluency, and then mode-specific edit cost.

Surface divergence uses target bands, not a reward to maximize. Pure mode penalizes
unnecessary change. Strong mode allows broader restructuring but keeps the same
fidelity floor and adds stricter document checks.

Ranking properties prove that no style or fluency gain can compensate for a hard
failure or lower semantic class.

Editorial-lint findings participate only after hard gates. Each contribution names
the exact rule and policy source. Watermark tests, provider classifiers, and generic
AI-authorship scores remain isolated diagnostics and cannot affect this ordering.

### 9A. Qualify editorial lint

- Freeze stable rule IDs, source ranges, explanations, severity, applicability,
  exclusions, proposed actions, and policy provenance.
- Add deterministic rules for conversational residue, canned transitions, repeated
  conclusions, inflated sectioning, vague attribution, redundant filler,
  punctuation density, and document-level phrase repetition.
- Protect quotations, citations, code, literal data, accessibility content, and
  intentional user-approved exceptions.
- Compare source and output with the same policy version and report resolved,
  retained, introduced, suppressed, and uncertain findings.
- Test each language and channel independently with human-written controls and
  adversarial near-matches.
- Require any learned rule to beat the transparent rule baseline without a material
  fidelity or false-positive regression.

The system reports named editorial findings, not AI authorship or universal source
classification.

### 10. Qualify unit and region atomicity

- Define independence and cross-reference graphs before allowing partial commit.
- Preserve failed units byte-for-byte.
- Rerun document-level validation after accepted edits are assembled.
- Detect inconsistent terminology, voice, numbering, references, and discourse.
- Report each committed and preserved region without printing content.
- Fall back to document-atomic output when independence cannot be established.
- Make partial atomicity explicit and keep document atomicity as the default for
  connected prose.

Cancellation or final verification failure discards all partial edits.

### 11. Qualify hierarchical documents and clarification

- Freeze the document map, source-linked guidance, unit identity, context budget,
  overlap, consistency, staging, and report contracts from
  [document transactions](../document-transactions.md).
- Test protected claims and cross-references at the beginning, middle, end, and unit
  boundaries at several context utilization levels.
- Prove that summaries and high-level plans cannot expand edit authority or replace
  source text as the fidelity reference.
- Prove one writer per eligible range, deterministic reassembly, stale-plan
  rejection, cancellation, and exact document or selection atomicity.
- Evaluate no questions, fixed questions, adaptive document questions, and a full
  brief under [the guided editorial brief contract](../editorial-brief.md).
- Report marginal question value, answerability, skip rate, burden, main-point and
  audience adherence, fidelity, and incorrect promotion to durable preferences.
- Keep a relational time-aware preference ledger as the baseline. A learned temporal
  graph must prove incremental value over it before becoming a supported strategy.

### 12. Harden with mutation, fuzz, and resource tests

- Mutation-test deterministic gates, ranking, router decisions, adapter verification,
  profile precedence, authorization, and atomicity.
- Repair weak assertions before setting the project mutation threshold.
- Extend fuzzing to every parser, deserializer, sentinel path, profile import,
  backend response, trace, and partial-commit planner.
- Stress bytes, tokens, candidates, units, concurrency, queues, retries, deadlines,
  decompression, and database work.
- Run cold, warm, repeated, long-input, and cancellation benchmarks on each declared
  hardware tier.

Resource regression thresholds are explicit and versioned. A faster result does not
justify a fidelity regression.

### 13. Qualify the portable runtime and multilingual matrix

- Package a pinned `llama-server` sidecar behind the runtime-neutral inference port.
- Start it offline with an exact model path and explicit context, sampling, KV-cache,
  device, and offload configuration. Disable automatic fitting and remote artifact
  shortcuts.
- Verify health, effective properties, tokenization, template, schema support,
  truncation state, and runtime identity before accepting output.
- Qualify CPU on every supported operating system, Metal on macOS, CUDA on Windows
  and Linux, and HIP, Vulkan, and hybrid paths only where they are advertised.
- Compare critical decisions across Ollama and llama.cpp for at least one controlled
  exact artifact. Divergence requires independent qualification or narrower support.
- Compare Q4 with Q8 or a higher-precision reference under a predeclared
  non-inferiority margin.
- Run independent native-authored and fluent-human-reviewed strata for English, at
  least one additional Latin-script language, and at least one non-Latin-script
  language.
- Test code switching, quotations, technical terms, identifiers, Unicode,
  directionality, and no-translation behavior. Ambiguity causes explicit language
  selection or abstention.

No resolver may silently change model, quantization, runtime, context, language,
strategy, or execution class after failure.

### 14. Run the locked qualification

- Freeze code, prompts, artifacts, evaluator policy, and manifests.
- Run every baseline and product strategy with its own frozen pre-registered policy,
  including candidate schedule when applicable, context and output bounds, seeds,
  evaluator cascade, and stopping rules.
- Report actual candidate use, tokens, latency, memory, and compute for every system.
  Define any normalized efficiency comparison before the run rather than calling
  unlike policies comparable after observing results.
- Blind and randomize owner-preference comparisons.
- Emit confidence intervals and per-category results.
- Publish abstentions, exclusions, invalid runs, and artifact drift.
- Review all critical false accepts before considering release status.
- Invalidate and rerun only through the documented suite-version process.

## Stop and rollback rules

- Remove a strategy if it adds no meaningful coverage or style gain.
- Raise abstention or narrow supported inputs if any critical risk category exceeds
  its predeclared bound.
- Replace or isolate an evaluator whose failure is too correlated with generation.
- Keep document atomicity if partial consistency cannot be established.
- Simplify the profile or retrieval path if its gain disappears under topic holdout.
- Reject a model or quantization that changes behavior outside its qualified bound.

## Required test matrix

| Area | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Governance | Split immutability, contamination checks, invalidation records, and reproducible manifests |
| Calibration | Reliability, risk-coverage, shift, missing-signal, and invalid-calibration behavior |
| Strategies | Shared cascade, mode eligibility, cancellation, budgets, and comparative gain |
| Router | Monotonic strictness, deterministic decisions, conflicts, and unavailable capabilities |
| Ranking | Hard-gate dominance, lexicographic ordering, divergence bands, and deterministic ties |
| Atomicity | Independence, cross-references, assembly, verification, cancellation, and byte preservation |
| Resources | Cold and warm runs, long inputs, concurrency, cancellation, memory, and platform variation |

Overall Rust line coverage remains at least 80 percent. Deterministic gates, ranking,
router, adapter verification, partial commit, and authorization target at least 90
percent and require meaningful mutation coverage.

## Exit evidence

The phase closes only when:

- Predeclared risk and coverage thresholds pass on the locked set.
- Every strategy uses the common cascade and shows measured value.
- Uncertainty and abstention remain first-class results.
- Partial atomicity is either qualified or remains explicitly unsupported.
- Mutation, fuzz, resource, cancellation, and cross-platform suites pass.
- Privacy and encryption claims match implemented behavior.
- All four refinement passes are complete with no open critical fidelity or
  calibration defect.

The handoff to 0.6 includes stable application outcomes, versioned wire-ready domain
contracts, calibrated policy identities, resource budgets, and shared conformance
fixtures that every interface must reproduce.
