# Milestone 0.2 generation qualification work package

## Status and objective

This is the post-admission trust-boundary work package for milestone 0.2. It
defines how one exact managed generation stack can produce candidates, bind those
candidates to local-judge execution, and earn a distinct generation qualification.

The outcome is deliberately narrow. It may authorize one exact generation tuple
only after fresh live revalidation. It does not prove semantic equivalence, factual
correctness, model-weight use, handler execution, or support outside the tested
language, mode, format, platform, and hardware envelope.

This plan is partly implemented. The current managed generation bracket and its
effective-state records are inert, and both production allowlists are empty. The
candidate side is implemented from planned attempt through exact completed-or-failed
record, successful receipt, opaque verified candidate batch and batch set, retained
exact case material, portable deterministic evaluation record, authoritative
deterministic compiler, and offline candidate-to-judge preparation. The preparation
join revalidates the exact retained authorities and derives the judge plan, two-order
schedule, request aggregate, and compatibility hard-gate projection without runtime
or network inputs. The app-owned managed-judge precursor, phase-ordered observer,
exact per-attempt request, response, complete residency-receipt, and effective-state
seal, plus the eval-owned pure output normalizer, exact no-launch handoff pairing,
and no-launch runner configuration authority are also implemented. The configuration
authority retains the complete named runner context and validates the exact one-model
seven-response preflight, caller ceilings, runtime, isolation, exact
launch-plan-to-model-package lease identity, model, judge-system, and
installation-generation closure without launching or connecting. The portable
schedule-indexed response record, its request-bound aggregate, observation identity,
managed-receipt relationship, and join validation are implemented, as is the
schedule-wide observation authority with its five evidence aggregates and dedicated
effective-state join. The distinct schedule-wide effective-package plan and release
are also implemented. They reject singular-attempt substitution, bind the exact
prepared-isolation subject and every real retained attempt state, and release the
complete observer authority only after cleanup plus all independent revalidations.
The single-entry managed schedule executor, cleanup-gated durable receipt, canonical
compatibility triage, opaque judge join, compact portable qualification record, and
eval-owned ordered live-repeatability join verifier are implemented. Canonical
app-owned platform and license assessment-policy authorities and assessment compilers
are implemented with empty production roots, as are the canonical privacy-bounded
host record, double-sampled current-host authority, typed generation-case request
profile, shared byte-exact prompt renderer, deterministic one-at-a-time request
builder, streaming projection authority, and structural model-license verification.
The eval-owned Draft -> Projected -> Prepared compiler captures one absolute deadline
before policy construction, atomically preregisters and canonically reads back the
schema-7 operation-policy and request-projection pair, and retains the exact
nonserializable app authorities without exposing launch or traffic. The consuming
Active owner is implemented for traffic-eligible Prepared states with one retained
process-local lifecycle and independent mandatory-finalizer validation. It is the
sole high-level strict judge entry, consumes exact preregistered repetitions in order,
and validates the complete operation scope before running the managed schedule under
the Prepared deadline. It is also the sole high-level strict candidate entry and
consumes target and baseline attempts in exact plan and request-projection order under
the same deadline and lifecycle. Target attempts require Approved resource
observation, baseline attempts use the compatibility observation shape, failure
consumes the ordinal and terminalizes without retry, and judging requires candidate
completion. Completed execution remains pending until its exact readback-verified
batch settles the durable ordinal. One process-local subject binds all Active
outcomes, batches, batch sets, paired judge handoffs, and joins. Target records are
retained in exact plan order with every completed target receipt. A one-shot target
attempt-ledger closure can be sealed only after exact receipt-to-record validation,
and judging also requires that sealed ledger. Rejected pretraffic terminalization and
fail-fast phase-policy refusal are implemented. Active-owned no-replace publication,
fresh readback, receipt compilation, exact failed-attempt derivation, and the
fresh-revalidatable in-memory operation interruption are implemented. Schema 10
preserves the schema 9 dependency-complete portable plan and case foundation and
recursive cold readback. Preregistration now materializes and retains that exact
foundation, and its schema 7 transaction cold-validates the same plan before either
preregistration record can commit. Active now requires a newly inserted exact
pretraffic precursor, owns pending completion, and closes completed or failed
candidate execution through schema 10. Completed closure binds app-root publication,

cohorts, final
`VerifiedGenerationQualification` compiler, activation, and live-use authority remain

The retained generation slice is implemented through inert state construction. The

## Why this is separate

The current boundaries do not form an exact qualification chain:

- The lower managed generation bracket binds one runtime process, both exact package
  leases, retained connection, private input tree, worker and native observations,
  request, response, portable effective state, attempt-bound live join, cleanup, and
  final package revalidation. The managed candidate wrapper adds the exact precursor,
  V2 evidence, publication, readback, receipt, and structured completed-or-failed
  outcome. Its completed capability can enter an opaque verified candidate batch and
  batch set, but it still has no managed judge join.
- `CandidateId` is a rewrite-unit and request-local ordinal. It is not unique
  across systems, qualification runs, or repetitions.
- The hybrid scorecard binds candidate byte digests, but its system identities are
  caller declarations.
- The local-judge receipt binds retained transport and request and response
  evidence, but not candidate-generation receipts or managed judge identity.
- Qualification v2 is exact, inert, and restricted to claim extraction. The
  qualification v1 activation route is too weak for generation.

Generation therefore needs its own records, joins, authorities, activation path,
and persistence tables. Do not widen qualification v2 or reinterpret an existing
receipt.

## Preconditions and dependency order

Implement and qualify the work in this order:

1. Preserve runtime admission and the separate exact package-and-worker generation
   review. Neither capability may substitute for the other.
2. Retain the completed private mount namespace, private procfs, and reviewed
   device view through every later operation before deriving CPU-only placement.
3. Retain the exact generation worker and every model-package member by file
   object. Reconstruct the reviewed Ollama manifest and blob layout inside a
   private read-only model root, then use a closed launch profile that points only
   to that root.
4. Discover the separate generation worker, require its exact retained executable,
   and bind its GGUF mapping to the retained model-weight object. This establishes
   bounded model-load evidence, not model-use or resident-page identity.
5. Complete direct generation-bound provider, effective output configuration,
   platform and driver, and compute-backend and placement evidence. Construct
   `EffectiveRuntimeState` only from app-observed records.
6. Retain and revalidate the exact `ModelPackageLease` across request construction,
   generation, final runtime observation, process cleanup, and result construction.
7. Construct effective-package evidence for the exact model artifact set, runtime
   build, and effective state.
8. Preserve the completed candidate-generation attempt and receipt records, opaque
   verified candidate batch and batch set, retained exact case material, and portable
   deterministic evaluation record and authoritative deterministic compiler.
9. Project the eight-case smoke and 39-case editorial protocol into immutable local
   generation plans without changing the source development corpora.
10. Preserve the managed candidate-to-judge preparation join, schedule-indexed
   response identity, and schedule-wide observation authority. Add the managed
   runner, cleanup-gated receipt, and opaque authority join. Keep the existing
   scorecard and local judge receipt at their current triage-only authority.
11. Add generation qualification, invalidation, activation, and live-use authority
   as a new domain. Deauthorize the legacy v1 generation route.
12. Run smoke, locked evaluation, repeatability, human adjudication, and
    supported-platform qualification in that order.

The contracts and fake-backed failure tests may land while production policies stay
empty. No real run may skip an earlier dependency.

The application now has four inert effective-state leaf records: retained provider
snapshot, version-scoped wire output configuration, bounded Linux platform and
native-framework observation, and bounded native-CPU execution evidence. The wire
leaf binds the reviewed v0.32.15 request mapping, including `num_gpu=0`, but
deliberately excludes the closed launch and private model-root relationships. It is
therefore not the complete effective configuration. CPU execution evidence requires
the private CPU device canaries, exact worker command profile with `-ngl 0`, the
accelerator-rejecting native-load observer, exact GGUF mapping, and zero
runtime-reported accelerator bytes. It remains bounded observation, not formal
placement proof. These serializable leaves grant no qualification or live-use

by their constructors.

The retained generation path now constructs the inert app-observed
`EffectiveRuntimeState` before cleanup. The live join reobserves the concrete model
isolation lease, cross-validates the admitted runtime, reviewed worker path, runtime
and model packages, closed launch, private input mapping, request, response,
residency, worker, GGUF mapping, platform, and CPU evidence, and is bracketed by
fresh runtime-package revalidation. Its portable state identity combines the
normalized actual server and worker native component sets, the complete closed
configuration, and the normalized worker output and execution configuration.

Attempt facts do not enter the portable state identity. Installation generations,
admission and frozen-discovery provenance, kernel and process observations, exact
request and response identities, residency, mapping object identities, and full
native observations remain in the app-owned live-join digest. Successful completed
authority is released only after process cleanup plus final model-package and
runtime-package revalidation all succeed.
`ManagedOllamaGenerationBracketObservationV1` binds this completed bracket and has an
empty effective-state missing set. Once a trustworthy typed bracket closeout exists,
an operation, cleanup, or revalidation failure instead produces a structured
non-success outcome with an exact portable failed record and retains its typed primary
cause and every cleanup or revalidation cause behind redacted debug output. Earlier
runner failures remain outside this guarantee. The original
`LocalOllamaManagedGenerationEvidence` version 1 schema and identity remain
unchanged. Managed-generation evidence version 2 implements the candidate path that
also binds effective-package evidence and the candidate-receipt precursor. None of
these records grants qualification or live-use authority, and none proves model use,
handler execution, resident-page identity, page immutability, driver absence, or
formal placement.

## Exact contract set

### Generation system identity

Add `GenerationSystemRecordV1` and a content-derived `GenerationSystemId` to
`rewrite-model`. Move no opaque authority into that crate. Where an existing fact
is currently exposed as an app-owned `Digest`, add an inert typed ID at the owning
boundary and preserve the old digest getter for compatibility.

The portable record uses `RuntimeAdmissionJoinId`, `ManagedGenerationPathId`, and
`FrozenExternalComponentSetId` wrappers owned by `rewrite-model`. Each wraps the
exact digest already derived by `VerifiedAdmittedRuntime`,
`VerifiedManagedGenerationPath`, or
`FrozenExternalNativeComponentSetId`, respectively, without rehashing. The owning
app or attestor converts from its live object; decoding the wrapper creates only an
inert value.

`GenerationSystemRecordV1` has these fields in this identity order:

1. `schema_version`.
2. `runtime_admission_join_id`, `managed_generation_path_id`, and
   `frozen_external_component_set_id`.
3. `runtime_package_manifest_id`, `runtime_build_id`, and
   `effective_runtime_state_id`.
4. `model_artifact_set_id`, `model_package_manifest_id`, `model_artifact_id`,
   `effective_package_evidence_v2_id`, and `static_model_binding_digest`.
5. `strategy_digest`, `planner_digest`, `validator_digest`, `adapter_digest`,
   `prompt_digest`, `output_schema_digest`, and `request_policy_digest`.
6. `language_digest`, `mode_digest`, `format_digest`,
   `operating_system_digest`, `architecture_digest`, `execution_class_digest`, and
   `hardware_envelope_digest`.

The record constructor validates the following relationships before deriving the
ID:

- The runtime build names the runtime package manifest.
- The effective runtime state names the runtime build.
- The model-package manifest names the model artifact set and contains the selected
  model artifact in its effective generation closure.
- Effective-package evidence V2 names the same model artifact set, runtime build,
  and effective runtime state.
- Every machine selector is nonempty, bounded, and canonical. Every policy digest
  is present, but remains an inert equality binding.

Derive `GenerationSystemId` as SHA-256 over
`retonr:generation-system-record:v1\0` followed by the canonical identity bytes in
the field order above. Installation generations, process facts, request and
response identities, and the app-owned live join are excluded. They describe one
execution of the portable system, not the stable tested subject.

A public decoded record is inert. The later app verifier must rederive every
cross-crate ID from the exact opaque runtime admission, generation-path, runtime
lease, model lease, and effective-state observations. Equality to caller-provided
digest text never creates authority.

### Canonical codec and typed live IDs

All new portable records use private fields, checked constructors, and strict
bounded codecs. Decode only after checking the raw byte ceiling. Reject duplicate,
unknown, missing, trailing, unsupported-version, and noncanonical JSON. Re-encode a
decoded record with the canonical serializer and require exact byte equality.

The identity encoder is independent of JSON object order. It appends fields in the
documented order, encodes `u32` and `u64` in big-endian form, enum variants as fixed
one-byte tags, typed IDs and digests as exactly 64 lowercase hexadecimal ASCII
bytes, strings and byte arrays with a big-endian `u64` length prefix, and optional
values with a zero or one tag. Arrays begin with a big-endian `u64` count. Checked
arithmetic is required before allocation or concatenation. The record-specific
domain prefix is prepended without a length prefix.

Debug output for these records exposes schema version, typed top-level IDs, counts,
and closed statuses only. It omits machine keys, source and prompt bindings,
candidate bytes, bundle paths, request and response material, host paths, process
facts, and installation details. No portable type implements an `authorizes`,
`approved`, `qualified`, or live-use method. No opaque authority has a raw-evidence
or raw-digest constructor.

Declare `ManagedOllamaEffectiveRuntimeStateJoinId` in `rewrite-model` as an inert
portable ID, and have `rewrite-app` derive it from the existing
`ManagedOllamaEffectiveRuntimeState::binding_digest()`. It wraps the exact digest
already produced under
`ollama/v0.32.15/live-effective-runtime-state-join/v1`; do not hash that digest
again and do not change the V1 calculation. Only the app-owned live-state builder
can derive it from live facts. A decoded copy is inert. Keep `binding_digest()` as
a compatibility view and add a typed getter for new code.

- `ManagedOllamaGenerationBracketObservationV1Id` for the existing bracket V1

### Attempt contract bounds and failure rules

Add these hard codec and evidence ceilings. A qualification plan may lower every
operational ceiling but may not raise one.

| Boundary | Hard maximum |
| --- | ---: |
| Machine key | 128 bytes |
| Generation-system, planned-attempt, precursor, cleanup, readback, or final-attempt JSON | 16 KiB each |
| Managed-generation evidence V2 JSON | 64 KiB |
| Evidence-bundle manifest JSON | 4 MiB |
| Evidence-bundle content entries | 4,096 |
| Canonical evidence relative path | 512 bytes |
| Aggregate generation evidence bundle | 256 MiB |

The existing limits of 16 candidates, 256 suite cases, 1,024 predeclared attempts,
4 MiB retained input, and 16 KiB candidate-receipt JSON remain hard ceilings.
Candidate ordinals are contiguous `u8` values. Attempt and repetition ordinals are
contiguous `u32` values within their owning plan or suite. Reject zero ceilings,
checked-count or byte-sum overflow, extra capacity hidden outside a plan, and any
record whose plan-local limit exceeds the hard contract.

Check cancellation before parsing and before each bounded filesystem or lease
operation. Reject oversized, malformed, unsupported, or noncanonical portable
input before expensive live revalidation. Once live work begins, preserve both the
primary error and any cleanup or final-revalidation error.

No output path issues a receipt when generation, cleanup, either final package
revalidation, publication, or readback fails. Cancellation and timeout after output
follow the same rule. A failed attempt still consumes its predeclared plan ordinal.
There is no retry under the same planned-attempt ID. A retry is a separately
predeclared planned attempt with a distinct ordinal and, when policy requires it, a
distinct seed.

## Attempt, cancellation, and cleanup semantics

- One attempt is one case, system, repetition, declared seed, and managed process
  bracket.
- There is no implicit retry. Repeatability uses separate predeclared attempt IDs.
- Failed, malformed, timed-out, and cancelled attempts count against the plan ceiling.
- Output observed before cancellation remains unusable until every postcondition and
  cleanup succeeds.
- Cleanup always uses a fresh cancellation token and retains both operation and
  cleanup errors when both fail.
- Cleanup failure aborts the complete qualification run and forbids later judge
  traffic.
- Runtime or model lease drift discards the complete attempt batch.
- Missing attempts, policy deviations, hidden retries, or incomplete evidence make
  the run ineligible for Qualified status.
- Judge execution remains exactly one attempt per case and order. Invalid output
  invalidates the retained session and stops without retry.

Source artifacts are immutable throughout. Failure, cancellation, timeout, and
cleanup never authorize source or output mutation.

## Formal and probabilistic limits

The contracts can deterministically enforce their defined structural properties:
exact identifiers, canonical records, bounded counts and bytes, request and response
joins, complete schedules, deterministic gate execution, observed process and
transport relationships, lease revalidation, and cleanup success.

Those properties do not formally prove:

- Semantic equivalence or factual correctness
- Complete claim extraction or clause alignment
- Generalization beyond the retained suite and exact tested envelope
- Causal use of the selected model weights or application handler
- Judge correctness, independence, or absence of correlated error
- Human authorship, ownership, disclosure compliance, or legal sufficiency

Judge output, claim comparison, calibration, repeatability, and human review remain
bounded probabilistic or adjudicative evidence. Deterministic hard gates are the only
machine acceptance authority. Human adjudication is release authority, not a formal
semantic proof. A Qualified record means that the exact stack passed the
preregistered policy on retained evidence under the named envelope.
